# 🎯 START HERE - Simple Steps

## ❌ YOU DON'T UPLOAD FILES ANYWHERE!

**Important:** There's NO website where you upload files. Everything happens from your terminal!

## ✅ What You Actually Do (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Login to Expo (in Terminal)

Open your terminal and run:

```bash
cd /Users/leohasid/locked-in-app
npx eas login
```

This will:
- Ask for your email
- Ask for your password (or help you create an account)
- Link your computer to Expo

### Step 2: Build Your App (in Terminal)

After logging in, run:

```bash
npx eas build --platform android
```

This command will:
- ✅ Automatically find your project files
- ✅ Automatically upload them to Expo's servers
- ✅ Build your app in the cloud
- ✅ Give you a link to download your APK

### Step 3: Download Your App

After the build finishes (10-20 minutes), you'll get a link to download your APK file.
