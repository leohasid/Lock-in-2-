# Apple Developer Account Setup Guide

## What You Need

### Apple Developer Program
- **Cost**: $99/year (USD)
- **What you get**: 
  - Publish apps to App Store
  - Use TestFlight for beta testing
  - Access to advanced features
  - Distribute to unlimited devices

### Free Apple Developer Account (Limited)
- **Cost**: Free
- **What you get**:
  - Test on your own devices (limited)
  - 7-day signing certificates (expire quickly)
  - Can't publish to App Store
  - Can't use TestFlight

## Step-by-Step: Sign Up for Apple Developer Program

### Step 1: Go to Apple Developer Website

**Visit:** https://developer.apple.com/programs/

### Step 2: Click "Enroll"

Click the big **"Enroll"** button

### Step 3: Sign In with Apple ID

- Use your existing Apple ID (the one you use for iPhone/iCloud)
- Or create a new one if you don't have one

### Step 4: Choose Account Type

**Individual** (most common):
- For personal apps
- Uses your name
- $99/year

**Organization**:
- For companies/businesses
- Requires business documents
- $99/year

**Choose "Individual"** unless you have a business.

### Step 5: Enter Information

You'll need:
- **Name**: Your full name
- **Address**: Your address
- **Phone**: Your phone number
- **Email**: Your email (already from Apple ID)

### Step 6: Payment

- **Cost**: $99 USD per year
- **Payment**: Credit card or PayPal
- **Auto-renewal**: Can be turned on/off

### Step 7: Verification

- Apple may verify your identity
- Can take 24-48 hours
- You'll get an email when approved

### Step 8: Activate

- Once approved, activate your account
- You're ready to publish!

## What You Can Do After Signing Up

### ✅ Publish to App Store
- Submit your app for review
- Make it available to everyone
- Get paid (if you charge)

### ✅ Use TestFlight
- Beta test with up to 10,000 testers
- Get feedback before launch
- Test on real devices

### ✅ Distribute to Devices
- Install on unlimited devices
- No 7-day expiration
- Full app capabilities

## Timeline

**Sign up**: 5-10 minutes
**Verification**: 24-48 hours (usually)
**Total**: 1-2 days to be fully active

## Cost Breakdown

- **First year**: $99
- **Renewal**: $99/year
- **Cancel anytime**: No refund, but can cancel auto-renewal

## Free Alternative (Limited Testing)

If you just want to test on your own iPhone:

1. **No account needed** for basic testing
2. **Use Xcode** (when you get access)
3. **7-day certificates** (expire quickly)
4. **Limited to your devices**

**But**: Can't publish to App Store or use TestFlight

## What You Need Before Signing Up

- ✅ Apple ID (free - you probably have this)
- ✅ Credit card or PayPal
- ✅ $99 USD
- ✅ Valid email address
- ✅ Phone number

## Quick Start

1. **Go to**: https://developer.apple.com/programs/
2. **Click**: "Enroll"
3. **Sign in**: With your Apple ID
4. **Choose**: "Individual"
5. **Fill out**: Your information
6. **Pay**: $99
7. **Wait**: 24-48 hours for approval
8. **Done!** Start publishing apps

## Important Notes

⚠️ **You need this to publish to App Store**
⚠️ **Required for TestFlight beta testing**
⚠️ **Needed for Codemagic builds (if publishing)**
✅ **One account = unlimited apps**
✅ **Can cancel anytime (no refund though)**

## After You Get It

Once you have your Apple Developer account:

1. **Add to Codemagic**: 
   - Go to Codemagic settings
   - Add your Apple Developer credentials
   - Codemagic will use it to sign your app

2. **Or use Xcode**:
   - Add your account in Xcode
   - Sign your app
   - Build and test

3. **Publish**:
   - Submit to App Store
   - Or distribute via TestFlight

1. Go to https://developer.apple.com/programs/
